<p><strong>ping</strong>命令一般用来测试两台机器或者机器和服务器之间网络是否连通。</p>
<p><img src="https://blogimage.musemvp.com/v1.0.0/qlrcf6/1603437323252-a3bf1470-c4ae-450a-b0fd-4d83281aab46.png" alt="" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p>
